# Screenshots to drop in before you present

Two slides ship with a sized, bordered placeholder box. Replace each box with a real
export — click the box, delete it, then Insert → Picture and size to the dimensions below.

| Slide | What to capture | Box size | Export at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 — "Your pipeline fills itself overnight." | "Companies to work — filled by agents today" with the drafted email panel open, showing **Send email / Discard / Save draft** | 8.76 × 2.95 in | 2× (≈1682 × 566 px) |
| 15 — Appendix / Market | no screenshot; insert real figures or delete the slide | — | — |

Notes on the capture itself:

- Crop out browser chrome, tabs and the URL bar entirely. The deck should show the
  product, not a browser.
- Shoot on a light background so it sits inside the deck's warm off-white without a
  visible seam.
- The approval buttons are the point of slide 8 — make sure they are in frame and legible
  at projector distance.
- If the aspect ratio doesn't match the box exactly, crop the screenshot rather than
  stretching it; distorted UI reads as sloppy from the back of a room.

## Before you present

- Slide 14: add your contact details under "Build it with us while it's still being shaped."
- Slide 15: fill in real, sourceable market figures and move it before slide 13 — or
  delete it. An invented TAM is the fastest way to lose a room that knows the market.
